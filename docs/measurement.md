# Proposed measurement contract — fictional demonstration v1

The browser event log is local scenario activity, not collected analytics or measured business improvement. Reset clears it. All real production payment outcomes require server-side verification; browser buttons are not evidence of money received.

Common production envelope: event ID, schema version, UTC occurred-at, source system, fictional/real environment flag, programme ID and optional pseudonymous session/enquiry/payment/campaign IDs. Restrict access and retention. Exclude beneficiary data, contact details and response text from analytics. Do not use an analytics event as the accounting ledger.

The demo records `payment_confirmed` both for a simulated donor outcome and for a manually recorded enquiry-linked receipt. They are labelled simulation; a production manual receipt needs a reconciled bank/provider reference and audit provenance. Preloaded receipts have no website donation-start event. Do not join them into a website conversion rate.

## Definitions and calculations

- **Confirmed programme funding:** sum of confirmed receipts matched to the programme. Pending, failed, cancelled, pledges and unmatched receipts are excluded. Remaining gap is `max(0, agreed budget - confirmed matched funding)`; excess is displayed separately.
- **Completed giving:** confirmed receipts, deduplicated by provider reference. Production must separately show refunds/chargebacks and distinguish confirmed cash from net settled funds. The demonstration has no refunds or settlement records.
- **Repeat support:** identified supporter IDs with at least two confirmed receipts in the same appeal dataset, divided by identified confirmed appeal supporters. Initial demo has one repeat supporter out of one identified appeal supporter (two receipts); selecting monthly is not evidence of a second payment. Production needs a stated reporting window and identity rules.
- **Enquiry follow-up:** addressed enquiries have a recorded response and owner; closed enquiries have a recorded response and closure status. Compare closed-with-response to total accepted enquiries. Sum separately confirmed receipts linked to enquiry IDs. A link denotes association, not causation.
- **Campaign comparison:** Learning appeal confirmed receipts minus KES 6,000 (KES 4,000 creative and KES 2,000 outreach). Payment fees, staff time and programme delivery costs are unknown/excluded, so this is a partial contribution-minus-campaign-cost figure, not overall net organisational income. Initially KES 60,000 - KES 6,000 = KES 54,000; after the seeded enquiry contribution, KES 90,000 - KES 6,000 = KES 84,000. Unmatched and unattributed receipts are outside appeal totals.
- **Reconciliation time:** production receipt timestamp to match timestamp; report median/p90 for matched receipts and count/age of outstanding receipts. Not measured by the demo: the initial receipt lacks a real provider timestamp.
- **Follow-up time:** production enquiry acceptance to first recorded response, plus due-date completion and time-to-close. The demo records response history timestamps and closure counts; it has no measured service-level improvement.
- **Programme comparison:** same 40-learner fictional cohort; 24/40 (60%) versus 32/40 (80%) meeting ≥3 of 4 weekly sessions every week for four weeks. Difference +8 learners, +20 percentage points, not “20% growth”. Sessions delivered (16 per period) are activities; attendance is an observed outcome proxy; learning attainment is unknown. October results remain unavailable publicly until a delivered review is approved; a delayed review must never publish the invented October attendance result.

## Assessment plan requiring KCH agreement

Agree a real baseline, reporting window, attribution/identity rules, full cost inclusions, confirmation/settlement policy and privacy controls before instrumentation. Compare confirmed giving and repeat support, follow-up completion and reconciliation age across equivalent periods. Account for campaign spend, offline contributions, seasonality and provider changes. Associations do not establish that website changes caused contributions or programme outcomes. A controlled evaluation would require a separate agreed design. No performance improvement is claimed here.
